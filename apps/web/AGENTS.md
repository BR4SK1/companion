# Web/BFF boundaries

- The web/BFF owns owner authentication and authorization at the request boundary. Keep credentials and session-handling secrets server-side; never expose them to browser or mobile clients.
- Treat domain identity separately from authentication identity. Do not propagate identity-provider subjects or speculative user/tenant IDs into domain records, jobs, or worker calls.
- Mobile and browser clients use the BFF boundary; preserve the single continuous visible conversation rather than exposing internal processing segments as separate threads.
- The non-production-only test-entry gate (`src/lib/server/test-gate.ts`, `server.mjs`, `server/`) is a second, loopback-only channel for E2E access. Keep it fail-closed (explicit enablement, never inferred from `NODE_ENV`; checks at listener bind, on every request, at issuance, and on every use), loopback-bound at the socket level with the process-random channel attestation, session-disjoint from the owner channel (dedicated secret and cookie, no identity), and free of any OIDC flow. The gate may open only under a valid deployment-purpose attestation: a declared `non-production` purpose in the one authorized non-production namespace (`companion-np`, pinned in chart and runtime) with the declared namespace equal to the downward-API pod namespace, or the explicit `WEB_TEST_GATE_TRUSTED_LOCAL` declaration honored only outside Kubernetes (no chart attestation present, no `KUBERNETES_SERVICE_HOST`/`KUBERNET_SERVICE_PORT` indicators) — production, unauthorized-namespace, unknown, missing, mismatched, and smuggled attestations are errors, never silent closes. The listener enforces channel integrity on every request: the Host header must be exactly `127.0.0.1:<gate port>` and forwarding/proxy headers are refused (DNS-rebinding defense; these checks reject only and are never an authorization factor, and Host/forwarding headers are never consulted for any decision). Never widen it (no configurable bind address, no Service or Ingress route) and never enable it in production values.
- Keep domain-owned types, schemas, and persistence with their owning domain. Treat framework and library choices as unresolved unless separately approved and recorded.
- Follow the root `AGENTS.md` for repository-wide scope, public-content, and delegation rules. Documented future designs do not by themselves authorize implementation.
